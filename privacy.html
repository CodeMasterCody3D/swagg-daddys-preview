Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers swaggdaddys.com and the Swagg Daddys Owner Android app, also identified as Swagg Daddys Mobile (package com.swaggdaddysmobile). Swagg Daddys Mobile Mechanics operates the service and handles customer privacy requests.

We use information you share to review vehicle problems, contact you, and arrange mobile mechanic service. Exact location sharing is optional. Requests and attachments are available through protected owner access.

Information the service handles

  • Service requests: your name, telephone number, vehicle, optional VIN and decoded vehicle specifications, city or area, urgency, requested service, problem description, and submission time.
  • Optional location: latitude, longitude, and estimated accuracy when you send a request or choose the website's location button and grant permission.
  • Optional media: photos and videos you select and submit, their filenames, types and sizes, and information or metadata contained in the submitted files. Large or alternate-format photos may be resized and converted in your browser before upload; conversions may remove metadata or animation. Avoid including information unrelated to your vehicle request.
  • Owner access: sign-in information used for authentication, session identifiers, job status, and private service notes. There is no public customer account signup.
  • Owner notifications: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging receives the registered owner's device identifier, customer name, and a short preview of the service request to deliver an alert. Phone numbers, GPS coordinates, and uploaded files are not included in that notification payload.
  • Security and technical information: hosting services may process IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and operational logs. The service uses IP-derived identifiers to limit abusive submissions and sign-in attempts.

Location, photos, and videos

GPS coordinates help identify where your vehicle is so the mechanic can reach you. The website asks for location permission when you send a request or select the location button, unless your browser already remembers your choice. You can decline and enter your city or area instead. The service does not continuously track your location in the background.

Coordinates and selected files are sent when you submit the request. Choosing a file does not itself grant GPS permission. Submitted media may already contain location metadata or show people and license plates. Revoking device or browser permission stops future permission-based access; it does not delete information previously submitted.

The owner app displays customer-submitted information. It does not request access to the owner's GPS, contacts, camera, microphone, call history, or message history.

VIN lookup, scanning, and vehicle history

VIN is optional. Entering a complete VIN or scanning a photo that yields one complete VIN sends it to NHTSA’s public vPIC service to retrieve manufacturer-reported specifications. NHTSA receives the VIN and connection information. This is a specifications lookup, not an ownership, accident, or service-history report.

The VIN-label photo reader first checks barcodes in your browser. If no complete VIN is found and the Google Lens reader is available, the VIN photo (or selected area when retrying) is automatically resized, converted to JPEG, and sent through our server to Google for text recognition. This behavior is disclosed beside the VIN photo buttons before you choose an image. Google receives that image and connection information from our server; Google's privacy policy applies. Service-request contact details and GPS coordinates are not sent to Google. If Google Lens is not enabled or cannot be reached during the availability check, printed text is read in your browser instead. Our server does not save the recognition image or raw recognition response. VIN-label photos are not attached to a service request. When you submit a request, its VIN and decoded specifications are saved privately with the request. Authorized owner access can link past requests, job statuses, completion dates, and service notes for the same VIN. This history contains only information recorded by Swagg Daddys.

Live VIN scanning asks for camera permission only after you select Live scan VIN. The camera preview remains inside the page. Barcode frames and, after repeated barcode misses, printed VIN text are processed locally on your device. The microphone is not requested. The camera stops after a confirmed VIN, when you stop it, when the page is hidden or left, or when you choose a saved photo. Selecting Take photo in the live view captures one image for the existing VIN-photo reader, including the disclosed Google Lens fallback if needed; live video is not streamed to Google or stored by our server. If in-page camera access is unavailable, a separate phone-camera option remains available.

Use and sharing

When an authorized owner chooses vehicle research, the server sends the VIN to NHTSA vPIC for specifications. Model year, make, model and selected configuration identifiers are used with NHTSA’s recall, safety-complaint and crash-rating APIs and FuelEconomy.gov’s EPA data. Customer names, telephone numbers, GPS coordinates, media and service notes are not sent with these research requests. Public responses may be cached in server memory for up to one hour. Model-level recalls and complaints are not an individual vehicle’s accident or completed-repair history. External theft, salvage and title-history checks open provider websites; the owner can copy and enter the VIN there. Provider fees, access limits and privacy policies apply.

We use requests, media, shared location, and notes to understand vehicle problems, prioritize requests, discuss availability and pricing, arrange service, and follow up. Authentication and technical information help operate and protect the service.

Netlify provides hosting, server functions, and private storage for records and attachments. Its infrastructure processes information on behalf of the service. Authorized business personnel and technical administrators may access information when needed to provide service, maintain the system, or handle a privacy request.

When you or the owner choose to call, text, or open Maps, the selected phone, messaging, or mapping provider receives information needed for that action, such as a telephone number, message, or coordinates. Its own privacy policy applies. The owner app does not automatically place calls or send messages.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The website and app code do not include advertising trackers. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Storage and security

Requests and uploads are stored on the backend, outside public website directories. Owner access requires authentication, and connections use HTTPS. An essential session cookie keeps the owner signed in; the owner session expires after 12 hours, and signing out invalidates it. The Android app retains its website session in private WebView storage and disables app backup.

No system can guarantee complete security. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, or sensitive information unrelated to vehicle service.

Vehicle repair-manual links

When the owner selects a VIN to find a repair manual, vehicle specifications are matched to LEMON Manuals’ directory. Operation CHARM is also available as a backup. The VIN is sent to NHTSA’s vehicle decoder to verify those specifications when the manual link is opened. Links to the manual providers use vehicle specifications such as the make, model year, model and engine description; the VIN and customer details are not included in those external links.

Retention and deletion

Requests, coordinates, uploads, and notes do not currently have an automatic deletion timer. They remain stored until the business or its technical administrator removes them. The owner’s Delete request button moves records to a recoverable Trash view; it does not permanently erase the record or its attachments. Trashed records are excluded from the active inbox and VIN history until restored. Information is kept for service requests, follow-up, necessary business records, and security.

To request access, correction, or deletion, call or text the number below. We may verify that the request concerns your information. We will arrange removal of information that is no longer needed, subject to records that must be retained for legal, security, or business obligations. Provider logs and backups may remain for their applicable retention periods.

Customers do not create an account to request service, and the owner app has no public registration feature. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete server-stored information.
