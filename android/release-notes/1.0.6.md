Install Swagg-Daddys-Owner-1.0.6.apk as an update to your existing owner app.

- **Stranded and urgent requests ring three times** (with a triple vibration) on their own "Stranded and urgent requests" channel, and say "URGENT" in the title.
- **Reminders until you open a request:** stranded/urgent every 15 minutes, everything else every hour ("Still waiting"). Opening the request, changing its status or moving it to Trash stops them.
- **Test sound** now lets you hear the normal ding or the urgent three rings.
- **Mileage from where you are:** the app can use your location (asked the first time) so a request shows driving miles to the customer and the $2/mile fee past 30 miles, and fills the invoice's mileage line.
- Keeps the GitHub APK updater, branded alerts and private lock-screen text from 1.0.5.

Website features that work in this app without an update: repair-manual finder (Tools), invoices with a text-to-customer link, and the faster VIN scanner.
